About

Hey! My name is Jules aka Nisk and I am an audio producer and engineer based in Bristol. I have a background in producing electronic music of all sorts of genres spanning from Dubstep to Garage to Jungle to anything in between. Most notably, I was awarded the number 1 spot on Keymag's 100 Best Garage Track of 2024 for a remix of a tune called 'Entreat' by the wicked experimental German artist 'Barcode'. Alongside this, I am a mixing and mastering engineer and produce audio for visual media. I love working with people who have strong creative visions and strive to make those visions come alive, so contact me to help with audio on your projects!
